# Putting the trip guide on your phone

The guide is a web page that behaves like an app once you add it to your
home screen. After that it opens instantly, works with no Wi-Fi or data,
and always lands on today.

Do this once, on Wi-Fi, before you leave.

## iPhone (Safari)

1. Open the link in **Safari** (not Chrome, not from inside Messages).
2. Tap the **Share** button (the square with an arrow, bottom middle).
3. Scroll down and tap **Add to Home Screen**.
4. Tap **Add** (top right).

A "The Trip" icon appears on your home screen. Open it once while still on
Wi-Fi so it finishes saving the photos.

## Android (Chrome)

1. Open the link in **Chrome**.
2. Tap the **three dots** (top right).
3. Tap **Add to Home screen**, then **Install** or **Add**.

## Using it

- It opens on **today**. Big arrows at the bottom go to yesterday and
  tomorrow; tap **Today** to jump back.
- **Map**, **Hotels** and **Info** are along the bottom.
- The phone number for Kensington's 24/7 help is under **Info**.
